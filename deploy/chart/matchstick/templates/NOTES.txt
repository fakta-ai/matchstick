matchstick runs as one replica: {{ include "matchstick.fullname" . }}.

Look at the pools:
  kubectl exec deploy/{{ include "matchstick.fullname" . }} -- matchstick status --config /etc/matchstick/matchstick.yaml

Drain a pool (until the pod restarts):
  kubectl exec deploy/{{ include "matchstick.fullname" . }} -- matchstick drain --config /etc/matchstick/matchstick.yaml --pool <pool>

Metrics and health: port {{ .Values.metrics.port }}, /metrics and /healthz.

config.claimURL must be https://<its address as the VMs reach it>:{{ .Values.claim.port }}/<path>; find the address with
  kubectl get service {{ include "matchstick.fullname" . }}-claim
